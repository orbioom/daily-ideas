- **Rill** — built — `03-rill` — hydration tracker with a smart goal engine and honest net-hydration coefficients (coffee/beer ≠ water) — *Monetization:* freemium, analytics free (opposite of WaterMinder), Pro for widgets/reminders — *Why it can boom:* top-grossing demand with concrete complaints (ads, paywalled stats, nagging) we directly answer.

- **Mise** — built — `06-mise` — recipe manager + meal planner whose **plan generates a consolidated grocery list** (the integration Paprika famously lacks), plus serving scaler & cook mode — *Monetization:* one-time purchase, buy-once no per-device fees — *Why it can boom:* beloved paid category with a famous unfixed core gap that Mise closes.

## Top recommendation

**Mise.** It targets a category with already-proven willingness to pay (Paprika, Mela, Crouton) and nails the single most-requested, still-unsolved feature — meal plan → consolidated grocery list — while undercutting the incumbent's per-device pricing. Clear wedge, clear buyer, low support burden (no accounts/servers). Runner-up: **Stint**, riding a wave of freelancers actively switching away from price-hiked incumbents.
