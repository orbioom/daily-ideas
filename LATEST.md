## Top recommendation

**Tessera** is the strongest broad-reach bet: 2FA is a universal, evergreen need, the incumbents are
genuinely weak, and a clean private offline authenticator with folders and biometric lock has obvious
mass appeal — and the OTP engine is RFC-correct here. For passionate, high-spending niches with no good
native incumbent, **Senpai** (anime/manga) and **Sillage** (fragrance) are the best "boom" plays. **Rook**
is the most technically impressive (a real, perft-verified chess engine) and rides a still-hot category.
