## Top recommendation
**Hush.** It targets the largest, most clearly top-grossing market of the six (sleep/white-noise), where the incumbents are actively resented for predatory subscriptions and feature-gating — a textbook "proven winner, mediocre incumbents" setup. Its core mechanic (fully on-device synthesis + a fade-to-silence timer + saved layered mixes) is both differentiated and cheap to operate (no audio hosting, no streaming, no account), which makes a fair price a real competitive weapon. **Chime** is the close runner-up for sheer wellness breadth and the cleanest "they paywall a countdown+bell" wedge; **Mettle** is the highest-velocity viral bet (75 Hard regularly trends).
