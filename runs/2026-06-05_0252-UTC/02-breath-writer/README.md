# Breath Writer

A writing environment synchronized to a breathing guide — the orb expands on inhale, contracts on exhale.

## The Idea

Most writing apps optimize for speed. Breath Writer does the opposite: it gives your body a rhythm
to follow while you write. The interface shows a softly breathing orb and labels each phase (inhale /
hold / exhale). The glass writing panel glows slightly on exhale — the moment when ideas are meant to flow.

Choose from five breathing patterns: box, 4-7-8, equal-breath, and two slow variants.
The app tracks words written, elapsed time, and breath count. Export as plain text when done.

## Why It's Interesting

There's real research connecting controlled breathing to reduced writing anxiety and improved focus.
This isn't a gamified productivity tool — it's a minimal writing environment that adds one sensory layer:
a shared rhythm between your body and the screen. The interface disappears; what remains is breathing
and words.

## How to Run

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install required. Click **begin** to start the breathing guide.

- Pick a pattern from the chips above the writing area
- The orb will guide inhale / hold / exhale
- The glass panel gets a subtle green edge glow on each exhale (your "write now" signal)
- **export .txt** downloads your session

## Patterns

| Pattern | Beats | Use for |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 4·4 equal | in 4 / out 4 | General writing, warm-up |
| 4·4·6 box+ | in 4 / hold 4 / out 6 | Sustained focus |
| 4·7·8 calm | in 4 / hold 7 / out 8 | Anxiety reduction, deep sessions |
| 5·5 slow | in 5 / out 5 | Flow state writing |
| 6·6 deep | in 6 / out 6 | Long sessions, slow thinking |

## Orbioom Feeling

Breathing orb using the silver radial gradient motif. Rings expand with inhale, the glass panel
uses the live green accent only for the exhale glow — restrained, purposeful. No timers, no
streaks, no score. Just breathing and writing in mist.
