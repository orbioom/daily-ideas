# Zettelkasten Method

The zettelkasten (German: slip-box) is a knowledge management method developed by sociologist Niklas Luhmann, who used it to write over 70 books and hundreds of articles.

## Core Principle

Every note is atomic — one idea per card. Notes link to other notes through explicit connections rather than folders or hierarchies. The system becomes a thinking partner over time, generating emergent insights from accumulated connections.

## Permanent vs Fleeting Notes

**Fleeting notes**: quick captures, processed within a day
**Literature notes**: what you read, in your own words
**Permanent notes**: distilled insights that connect to the broader web of ideas

The goal is to write permanent notes as if writing for your future self who has forgotten the context entirely.

## Why It Works

The physical act of linking forces you to understand both notes well enough to articulate the connection. Unlike folders (which are filing systems), the zettelkasten is a thinking system.
