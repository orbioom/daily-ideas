# Writing for Clarity

Clear writing is clear thinking made visible. The act of writing forces precision in ways that
thinking alone cannot — you can't hide vague ideas in a sentence the way you can hold them vaguely in mind.

## Principles

**One idea per sentence.** Long compound sentences dilute the main point. When in doubt, split.

**Active voice.** Passive constructions obscure agency. "The window was broken" hides who broke it.

**Concrete nouns.** "The dog barked" is stronger than "the canine vocalized." Specificity creates
pictures; abstractions demand work from the reader.

**Cut mercilessly.** First drafts are thinking aloud. Good prose is what's left after everything
unnecessary is removed. Kill your darlings.

## The Revision Process

Write to discover. Rewrite to communicate. These are different cognitive modes.
Most writers skip revision, which is why most writing is worse than it needs to be.

Read aloud to catch rhythm problems. If you hesitate while reading, the reader will hesitate too.

## On Style

Style is not decoration added on top of content — it is inseparable from meaning. How you say
something changes what you say. Hemingway and James are writing about the same world; they are
not saying the same things.
