# Hush

**Hush** is a sleep-sounds & white-noise mixer for iOS where **every sound is synthesized on your device in real time** — there are no audio files anywhere. The app is tiny, works fully offline (even in airplane mode), and lets you layer and mix sounds infinitely. It's a one-time purchase, calm, nocturnal alternative to the subscription sleep apps that charge ~$60/yr and lock their libraries.

## What it is

A native SwiftUI app (iOS 17+) built around a real-time DSP audio engine. You toggle synthesized sounds — white/pink/brown noise plus rain, ocean, wind, stream, fan, campfire and night crickets — blend their volumes into a mix, save your favorite blends, and fall asleep to a gentle sleep timer that fades to silence and keeps playing in the background.

## Full feature list

- **Real-time synthesis engine** — one `AVAudioEngine` + a single `AVAudioSourceNode` whose render block sums every active generator (scaled by gain), with a soft limiter and hard clamp to [-1, 1]. No audio files; the whole library is generated by DSP.
- **10 distinct generators** — White, Pink (Paul Kellet filter), Brown (leaky integrator), Rain (high-passed noise + randomized droplet bursts), Ocean (brown noise amplitude-modulated by a slow swell LFO), Wind (band-passed noise with a random gust LFO), Stream (bright bubbling noise + pitched burbles), Fan/Hum (cascaded low-pass + faint 120 Hz motor hum), Campfire (low rumble + random crackle pops), Night Crickets (gated high-frequency chirps over a faint bed).
- **Mixer screen** — adaptive grid of sound tiles, per-layer volume sliders for active sounds, master volume, a big play/pause control, a live now-playing summary, save-as-mix, and an empty state. Free users are capped at 3 layers with a Pro nudge.
- **Mixes screen** — built-in preset mixes (Rainstorm, Ocean Night, Deep Focus, Cozy Fan, Forest Stream, Campfire Night, White Room) plus your saved mixes; tap to load & play; rename, delete, duplicate, and favorite; empty state for saved mixes.
- **Timer screen** — duration presets + fade-length control, plus a full-screen countdown ring (driven by a stored end `Date` + `TimelineView`, so it survives backgrounding) with a fade-out indication, add-15-minutes, and cancel. Reduce-Motion drops the glow to a still ring.
- **Sessions screen** — listening history with Swift Charts: a minutes-per-night `BarMark` chart, most-used sounds, total hours, average session, and a current night streak. Empty state.
- **Settings** — default timer duration, default fade length, haptics toggle, free-tier layer/saved-mix limits, a background-audio note, and About. (≥3 real persisted preferences.)
- **Paywall** — one-time Hush Pro unlock/restore.
- **First-run onboarding** gated by a persisted `hasOnboarded` flag.
- **Crash-proof & accessible** — calm recoverable states for store/audio-session failure, full Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels/values on tiles, sliders and charts, WCAG-AA contrast in light and dark, sparse haptics gated by a toggle.

## Run steps

1) `brew install xcodegen` (one-time).
2) In `ios/`, run `xcodegen generate` (or run `./gen.sh` at the repo root).
3) Open `Hush.xcodeproj` in Xcode 15+, select an iOS 17+ simulator, Cmd+R.

**Free-signing:** set your Apple ID team in Signing & Capabilities; the bundle id is `com.orbioom.hush`.

## Tech notes

- **iOS 17+**, **SwiftUI 5**, `NavigationStack`, `TimelineView`, two-parameter `.onChange(of:)`.
- **AVAudioSourceNode real-time DSP synthesis** — pure value-type generator structs with a fast seeded RNG (SplitMix64) and one-pole filters; the render closure is allocation-free and index-guarded, reads live per-generator gains via an `os_unfair_lock`-guarded `Params` snapshot, guards every `AudioBufferList` buffer pointer via `UnsafeMutableAudioBufferListPointer`, and copies into reused scratch buffers. **No audio files** — the app ships zero sound assets.
- **MVVM** — one observation pattern app-wide: `@Observable` (`SoundEngine`, `SleepTimer`, `AppSettings`, `ProStore`) stored as `@State` and shared through the environment; pure aggregation helpers (`SessionStats`, `MixLoader`) kept separate from views.
- **SwiftData** — `SavedMix` (cascade-owning `MixLayer`) and `ListeningSession`, all registered in the `Schema`; small flags/prefs via `@AppStorage`/`UserDefaults`. Survives relaunch; seeded idempotently with starter mixes and ~5 weeks of nightly sessions.
- **AVAudioSession** is configured `.playback` so audio continues through the silent switch and in the background.
- **Design language** — dark-first, calm/nocturnal: a teal accent (#2E8E9E), deep-ink surfaces, soft glows, gentle rounded cards. Light mode is first-class via an adaptive `HushTheme`.
- **Monetization:** one-time **$3.99** Hush Pro (full generator library + unlimited layers + unlimited saved mixes + long/custom timers & fades + all presets). The core noises, three presets, three layers and the basic timer are free forever.
- **Why it can boom:** incumbents charge ~$60/yr subscriptions and lock their sound libraries; Hush synthesizes everything on-device, so it's a sub-megabyte, fully-offline app with an infinite, mixable library for one tiny one-time price.
