Algorithm Comparison

Algorithm: A* Best-First Search

A* selects cells to expand by their f-score: f = g + h, where g is the cost from start and h is a heuristic estimate to the goal. The open set is stored in a binary min-heap for O(log n) extraction.

Heuristics implemented

Manhattan (4-conn), Euclidean, Chebyshev, Octile (8-conn), Zero (Dijkstra). An admissible heuristic never overestimates; A* with an admissible h is guaranteed to find an optimal (minimum-cost) path.

Variants

Dijkstra = A* with h=0 (uniform cost, always optimal). Greedy Best-First = f=h (fast but not optimal).

Primary Citation

Hart, P. E.; Nilsson, N. J.; Raphael, B. (1968). "A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths." IEEE Transactions on Systems Science and Cybernetics, SSC-4(2): 100–107. DOI:10.1109/TSSC.1968.300136

Honest notes

With weighted terrain, Manhattan/Euclidean remain admissible only when the minimum terrain cost is ≥ 1. Diagonal moves cost √2 × terrain. Corner-cutting prevention is strict (both adjacent cardinal neighbors must be clear). Grid bounded at 50×50 for animation performance.
