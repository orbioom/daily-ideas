# Oche

**A calm darts companion — checkouts, matches, and double practice.**

Oche (the throw line in darts) helps a 501 player in three ways: it solves any
checkout, it logs matches leg-by-leg into real stats, and it drills the doubles
you keep missing. Built for league and pub players who want the numbers without
the clutter. Everything is on-device — no account, no ads.

## Features

- **Checkout solver** — enter any score from 2 to 170 and the number of darts in
  hand; Oche returns the conventional finishing route, always ending on a double
  or the bull, plus alternates. A bogey number (159, 162, 163, 165…) is named as
  such instead of failing silently.
- **Full checkout chart** — every finish from 170 down to 2, generated by the same
  engine, as a scrollable reference.
- **Match log** — record matches (301/501/701, best-of-3/5/7) leg by leg: darts
  thrown, the double you finished on, double attempts, and your highest visit.
  Full CRUD on matches and legs.
- **Live stats** — three-dart average, checkout %, best leg, highest visit, and
  win rate, computed per match and across your whole record.
- **Double practice** — pick a target double, log hits and misses with big Hit/Miss
  buttons, and Oche surfaces your weakest finish so you know what to drill.
- **Insights** — career average, a per-match average trend chart (Swift Charts),
  checkout %, and records.
- **Settings** — default start score, match length, alternate-routes toggle, and
  haptics; replay intro; clear all data.

## The engine

`CheckoutEngine` is a pure value-type solver. It searches conventional setup darts
(heavy trebles first, then tidy singles, then the bull) for the minimal-dart finish
that lands exactly on a legal double — the way the maths actually plays at the
board. `Dart` models every ring (single/double/treble/outer/bull) with correct
scoring and a finishing flag.

## Run it

1. `brew install xcodegen` (one-time).
2. In `ios/`, run `xcodegen generate`.
3. Open `Oche.xcodeproj` in Xcode 15+, select an iOS 17+ simulator, Cmd+R.

Free-signing: select your personal team under Signing & Capabilities; no paid
account needed to run on a simulator or your own device.

## Tech notes

iOS 17+, SwiftUI 5, MVVM-ish with a pure engine off the view layer. SwiftData
(`Match` → `Leg`, plus `PracticeSession`) for persistence that survives relaunch;
`UserDefaults`/`@AppStorage` for small prefs. Orbioom design language throughout:
liquid glass via `.ultraThinMaterial`, ink-gradient primary buttons, SF Pro with
monospaced figures, light + dark first-class, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels,
Reduce Motion, and sparse gated haptics.
