# Snowline

**Find the bottom of the hill.** A debt payoff planner that simulates avalanche vs snowball month by month, gives you a real debt-free date, and shows exactly how much interest and time each strategy saves.

**Audience:** anyone juggling cards and loans who wants a clear, private payoff plan — no bank login, no upsell.

**Design language:** Orbioom.

## Features

- **Debts** — full CRUD. Each debt has a type, balance, APR, minimum payment, and an include-in-plan toggle. Totals strip (owed, min/month, weighted average APR) and a "minimum < interest" warning flag.
- **Debt detail & payments** — log payments (which lower the balance and keep a history); delete a payment to restore the balance. Shows monthly interest and APR.
- **Plan** — choose avalanche or snowball, slide an extra monthly payment, and see your debt-free date, payoff time, interest paid, and total paid. A payoff-order list shows when each debt clears.
- **Month-by-month schedule** — the full amortization table (paid / interest / ending balance per month) generated by the simulation engine.
- **Compare** — avalanche vs snowball vs minimum-only side by side, with interest saved and months saved versus the baseline.
- **Settings** — currency (10 options), default strategy, haptics; reload sample data; delete all (confirmed).
- The engine accrues monthly interest, pays minimums, then funnels freed minimums + extra to the strategy target (true debt-snowball rollover), detects "stuck" debts where minimums can't outpace interest, and caps at 50 years. Onboarding gate, empty/loading/success/error states, light + dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion.

## Run it

1. `brew install xcodegen` (one-time).
2. In `ios/`, run `xcodegen generate`.
3. Open `Snowline.xcodeproj` in Xcode 15+, pick an iOS 17+ simulator, **Cmd+R**.

**Free signing:** Signing & Capabilities → your personal team.

## Tech notes

iOS 17+, SwiftUI 5. Persistence is **SwiftData** (`Debt`, `Payment`); prefs in `@AppStorage`. The payoff simulation is a pure, value-type `PayoffEngine` (no SwiftData dependency), so it's testable and never blocks the UI. Currency formatting via `NumberFormatter`. No dependencies, no network.
