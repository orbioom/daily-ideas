Method & references

The power spectrum is computed with the radix-2 iterative Cooley–Tukey FFT: bit-reversal permutation followed by log₂N butterfly stages, in pure JavaScript (no library). The one-sided power spectral density is PSD[k] = |X[k]|² / (Fs · Σw²), doubled for interior bins so total power is preserved.

Windows (rectangular, Hann, Hamming, Blackman) are applied before the transform to reduce spectral leakage. The spectrogram is a Short-Time Fourier Transform: a sliding window of the chosen size and overlap, FFT per frame, magnitude in dB stacked as a viridis-like heatmap.

Cooley, J. W. & Tukey, J. W. (1965). "An algorithm for the machine calculation of complex Fourier series." Mathematics of Computation 19, 297–301.

Harris, F. J. (1978). "On the use of windows for harmonic analysis with the discrete Fourier transform." Proceedings of the IEEE 66(1), 51–83.

Welch, P. D. (1967). Averaged periodogram method — the STFT frames here are the same windowed sub-records Welch averages.
